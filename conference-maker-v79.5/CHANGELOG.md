# Conference Maker v79.5

Completed fixes from the v79.4 host testing pass.

- Setup Conference team entries and People now share one synchronised person record, including convenors, administrators, coordinators, graphic designers, web designers and custom team roles.
- Conference Convenors, DHS people and JdH people are automatically included in Academic Committee and Chairs, with explicit removal retained as an editable opt-out.
- The Chair of the JdH Editorial Board is ordered first in the JdH group.
- Generated interface surfaces now choose light or dark foreground text automatically using WCAG relative luminance.
- Programme and Conference Guide tabs interrupt the content-card hairline and visually attach to the sheet below.

# Conference Maker v79.4

Corrective UX build after v79.3 testing.

- People are readable cards by default. A small Edit button opens fields for one person only; Save/Cancel returns the card to read mode.
- Conference Information has explicit Edit buttons beside active Guide sections and scrolls/focuses the relevant editor.
- Papers tray uses a real browser popup window, with a clear popup-blocked message.
- Floating Designer preview is draggable by its header.
- Three Keynotes are enforced as active defaults.
